**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Innocent Assault SS**. ## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Airi explores three-dimensional dungeon maps with direct character control. Use normal movement to navigate corridors and platforms, then combine the double jump and dash for vertical traversal, evasive movement, and access to elevated or hidden routes. The trainer can reduce the risk of damage while learning unfamiliar layouts.

### Tools & Inventory

Exploration rewards include weapons, skins, upper and lower body outfits, arm and leg equipment, back accessories, and underwear. Gems obtained from completed stages are used for upgrades. Equip collected items between missions and use the trainer's progression options to test different equipment and ability combinations.

### World & Environment

The game includes eight main stages and five challenge stages, divided into exploration areas and boss areas across 35 maps. Dungeons contain hidden paths, collectibles, monsters, traps, special items, turrets, shells, and healing spaces. Search each area carefully before entering the boss section.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat is built around switching between close-range sword techniques and long-range firearms. Change modes according to enemy type, distance, and terrain. Combine mouse clicks, charged inputs, and directional commands to perform stronger attacks. Firearms include auto-aim support, while restraint-capable enemies and traps can trigger defeat sequences if Airi is captured.
